About me

My research focuses on bioinformatics, genetics, and integrative multi-omics, with an emphasis on the computational aspects of interdisciplinary research that combines experimental and computational approaches. I am particularly interested in leveraging large-scale genomic and multi-omics datasets to investigate genotype–phenotype relationships and uncover the genetic and molecular mechanisms underlying complex biological traits. In parallel, I am interested in developing bioinformatics tools and computational methods to facilitate the analysis, integration, and interpretation of high-dimensional biological data. Through the integration of computational analysis, tool development, and experimental research, I aim to bridge the gap between data-driven discovery and biological validation.

Academic experience

I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees from the College of Life Sciences at Shanghai Normal University. News

-- Sep 2026 --
My recent research focuses on deep learning and large-scale foundation models for genomic feature learning. In particular, I am interested in developing computational models to characterize the regulatory logic of alternative splicing and to better understand how genomic sequence features contribute to transcript isoform diversity.

-- Mar 2026 --
Our collaborative study, “Targeting E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Hrd1 Prevents Myocardial Ischemia-Reperfusion Injury Through Enhancing ALDH2 Enzymatic Activity,” was published in Circulation. I am a co-first author of this work and was responsible for the bioinformatics analyses presented in the study. This work uncovers a previously unrecognized mechanism by which Hrd1-mediated ubiquitination regulates ALDH2 activity and myocardial ischemia-reperfusion injury.

-- Sep 2025 --
I have been admitted to the Ph.D. program at Zhejiang University and will pursue my doctoral research in Prof. Ronggui Hu’s laboratory at Liangzhu Laboratory.
